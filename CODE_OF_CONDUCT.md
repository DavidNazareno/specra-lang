# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want Specra to be a constructive, respectful project where people can discuss language design,
verification, and AI workflows without hostility.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- giving actionable technical feedback
- assuming good intent
- discussing ideas without attacking people
- being clear when something is experimental or uncertain

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment or intimidation
- insulting or dismissive language
- bad-faith argumentation
- publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or limit participation when behavior
harms the project or its contributors.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and related project
spaces.
